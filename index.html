Give your class its own economy

In 29 World™, students earn classroom dollars, pay rent, trade with classmates and save for a goal. Teachers choose the jobs, prices and paydays. It’s free for every class.

Pick the activities you want to teach

Every class runs on the same economy. Teach the parts your students are ready for.

  • Jobs and wages

    You choose the jobs and paydays. Students earn their wage.

  • Rent and bills

    Homes and fares come out of every payday, so budgets matter.

  • Saving and KiwiSaver

    Students decide what to put away for later.

  • Shares and property

    Students can grow their savings by investing.

How a class works

  1. Set up the classChoose a template with jobs, homes and prices already set, or create your own.
  2. Bring students inShare the class code so students can join, or create their accounts together from the teacher dashboard.
  3. Run a paydayStudents check their wages, cover their bills and choose what to do with the balance. Teachers can follow their progress in class reports.

Free for everyone

29 World™ is completely free for students, teachers and schools of any size. There are no subscriptions or per-student fees.

Help cover the database costs

Every class uses the database to save balances, paydays and purchases. Donations help pay that bill. If you’re bringing a large group or a whole school, please contribute if you can. Donations are always optional.
