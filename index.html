How a class works

  1. Set up the class. Choose a template with jobs, homes and prices already set, or create your own. Pick the activities you want to teach.
  2. Bring students in. Share the class code so students can join, or create their accounts together from the teacher dashboard.
  3. Run a payday. Students check their wages, cover their bills and choose what to do with the balance. Teachers can follow their progress in class reports.

Free for everyone
