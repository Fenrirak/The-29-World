Pricing & donations

Free for everyone.

Bring one class or your whole school. Every learner gets full access to 29 World™ at no charge.

Class balances and purchases live in a database that costs money to run. We rely on donations to help cover that bill. If you’re bringing a large group, please contribute if you can. Giving is always optional.
