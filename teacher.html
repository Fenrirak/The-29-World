Lifestyle rating settings

The lifestyle rating (an uncapped score starting at 0, shown on every student's homepage) is built from stars: the comfort rating of each property a student owns, their single comfiest vehicle, and any stars you gave a store item or insurance plan. Loans and credit card debt can take points off. While property scoring is on, a student with nowhere to live (no home of their own or rented) is always at 0. Set how many points each star is worth, and toggle sections on or off.

Lifestyle rating bands

Give ranges of the score meaningful names, e.g. "Poor" for 0-10, "Good" for 10-20. The score has no upper limit, so a student at or above the top of your last band still gets that last band. Bands should be ordered lowest to highest and cover the range you want labelled. You can also set extra requirements a student must meet to actually be shown a band, e.g. a minimum net worth, or owning a property/vehicle with at least a certain comfort rating, even if their score alone is high enough. Leave a requirement at 0 to not require it. If a student's score qualifies for a band but they don't meet its requirements, they'll be shown the next band down that they do qualify for.

Lock modules by lifestyle rating

Stop students from using certain modules while their lifestyle rating is low. Pick a cutoff and tick which modules should be locked when a student is at or below it.

There's a second, independent way to lock a module: set a short quiz a student has to pass first. That lives on the Quizzes page, along with its own on/off switch, a module locked by either system stays locked until both are satisfied.
