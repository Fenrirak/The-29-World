Lifestyle rating settings

The lifestyle rating (an uncapped score starting at 0, shown on every student's homepage) is built from stars: a property's comfort rating, and any stars you gave a store item or insurance plan. Set how many points each star is worth, and toggle sections on or off.

Lifestyle rating bands

Give the 0-100 score meaningful names, e.g. "Poor" for 0-10, "Good" for 10-20. Bands should be ordered lowest to highest and cover the range you want labelled. You can also set extra requirements a student must meet to actually be shown a band — e.g. a minimum net worth, or owning a property/vehicle with at least a certain comfort rating — even if their score alone is high enough. Leave a requirement at 0 to not require it. If a student's score qualifies for a band but they don't meet its requirements, they'll be shown the next band down that they do qualify for.

Lock modules by lifestyle rating

Stop students from using certain modules while their lifestyle rating is low. Pick a cutoff and tick which modules should be locked when a student is at or below it.

There's a second, independent way to lock a module: set a short quiz a student has to pass first. That lives on the Quizzes page, along with its own on/off switch — a module locked by either system stays locked until both are satisfied.

Random weekly events

Every week, each student gets 2 to 5 of the active events below, each on a different school day (Monday to Friday, never two on the same day). The week is planned the first time you open the site that week, so opening it on Monday gives the full spread. Events without "Can repeat" are only ever given once to each student, so add plenty of events or tick "Can repeat".
