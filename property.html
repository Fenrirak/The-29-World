Mortgages

Weekly due day Financed properties owe their weekly payment on this day (the week of purchase is free). Students pay it themselves — if it's still unpaid once the day passes, a red "payment overdue" warning shows on their profile.
Mark this week's payments as due now Overrides the due day for the rest of this week only, so students can pay right away. Doesn't charge anyone automatically, and turns itself off when the week ends.
Moving cost One-off fee when a student moves into a new home (their own, a classmate's, or a school rental). 0 = free. Students can only move once per day regardless.
Break fee Flat fee added to the payoff when a student sells back a property that still has a fixed-rate mortgage. 0 = none. Variable mortgages and mortgage-free properties never charge it.
Variable rates Each listing with a variable range has a floating rate that moves by itself once a week (the first page load of the week triggers it) and stays inside that range. Set the range in the listing's Financing section. Use this to move every rate one step right now — handy for showing the class what a rate change does to repayments.

Daily price movement
