Tax

Set a tax rate on different parts of the class economy. Purchases get taxed on top of the price; income gets taxed by withholding a percentage. Store prices and insurance premiums have no extra tax — the price shown is what students pay.

Wage tax brackets

Set different income tax rates for different wage levels, like real tax brackets. Each bracket only taxes the portion of a wage that falls within it — earning more never lowers what you keep from the money you already earned.
