Unlock gates

Attach a quiz to a module and, while the quiz is Active, that module is locked: a student can open it, but a quiz shows instead and nothing in the module is usable until they pass. Paused quizzes lock nothing. Quizzes with no module attached are practice only. Set a deadline on a quiz and you'll be notified when students miss it.
