Loan settings

Set up price ranges, each one has its own term length and interest rate. A student picks an amount, and whichever range it falls into decides their terms. Interest is charged the moment a loan is taken out, then compounds again every Monday for as long as it stays unpaid, the term length is shown to students as the loan's due date, but doesn't cap how long they can take (or stop interest from adding up while they do).
