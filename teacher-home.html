    Share as a template

    Anyone with this link can use 's settings — jobs, properties, side hustles, the store, and every other class setting — as a starting point for a brand new class in their own account.     Delete my account

    This is permanent and cannot be undone.

    If you continue, the following will be deleted straight away:

    • Every class you own, including its settings, balances, transactions and all activity history
    • Every student account in those classes, and any family links and shared template links for them
    • Your recovery email, if you set one
    • Your teacher login. You won't be able to log in again, and your username becomes free for someone else to use

    Classes where you are only a co-teacher are not deleted; you'll simply stop being part of them. If you only want to pause a class, use Archive instead. Nobody, including the developer, can restore a deleted account.
