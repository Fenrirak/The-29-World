Pay day

The day of the week wages are due. They’re paid automatically whenever that day comes around, to every student with a job. Task jobs need your tick; check-in and questionnaire jobs pay automatically, in proportion to the days logged (3 of 5 days = 3/5 of the wage), or use Run Pay Day on your Dashboard to pay early.

Side hustles

Students each pick one hustle and a daily check-in hour (e.g. 1am, 2am, 3am...). They must check in within 15 minutes after that hour to get paid, the amount depends on which hour they picked. Once a student has picked one, any change needs your approval, that stops someone quietly retiming their check-in to match whenever they happen to be online.

Tax on my pay

Tax (PAYE) comes off each pay before it reaches you. A rate only applies to the slice of your pay inside its band, so earning more never lowers what you keep from the money you already earned.
