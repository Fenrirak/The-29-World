2. Play money only

Everything in the app that looks like money, including balances, wages, savings, loans, term deposits, KiwiSaver, property, shares, insurance, tax and any prizes, is play money used for learning.

  • Play money has no real-world value. It cannot be bought, sold, withdrawn, cashed out or exchanged for real money, goods or services.
  • Rates, prices, events, taxes and rules in the simulation are simplified and invented for teaching. They are not a forecast of real markets.
  • Nothing in the app is financial, investment, tax, legal or gambling advice. Do not rely on it to make real financial decisions.
  • Any games of chance in the app, such as the casino-style mini-games (which your teacher can switch on or off), use play money only and are there to teach about risk and odds. Winning or losing in them has no real-world consequence, and they are not an encouragement to gamble with real money.
