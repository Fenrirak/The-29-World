Events

Random events

Every week, each student gets 2 to 5 of the active events below, each on a different school day (Monday to Friday, never two on the same day). The week is planned the first time you open the site that week, so opening it on Monday gives the full spread. Events without "Can repeat" are only ever given once to each student, so add plenty of events or tick "Can repeat".

Big events

Rare, random events with high stakes. Each week every student has a 30% chance of being hit with one, on a random school day (Monday to Friday). When it happens they must choose to pay the cost, lose the associated thing, or claim matching insurance. Insurance is only claimed when the student chooses it.

Normally each eligible student has a 30% chance of getting one per week, on a random school day. This gives one to every eligible student right now (someone with a job/property/vehicle depending on the event), skipping that chance. Anyone whose big event for this week is still waiting for its day gets it now instead; anyone who's already had this week's is skipped.
