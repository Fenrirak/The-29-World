If something can’t be reached

  1. Wait a minute and check again. Short drops often clear by themselves.
  2. Try another network. If the site works on mobile data or at home, the first network is probably blocking it. School and work networks do this most often.
  3. Try another browser or a private window. Extensions that block ads or trackers can stop sign-in and saving.
  4. Ask school IT to allow the addresses listed below.
  5. Still stuck? Read the technical setup help, or contact support with the page, device, browser and what this page showed.

For school IT

Questions

Is 29 World™ down for everyone?

This page can only test from your device. If every service shows Working but a page still won’t load, reload it. If services show Can’t reach on several devices and networks, the problem is probably with Google or GitHub, which run sign-in, saving and hosting. You can check Google Firebase status and GitHub status.

What do Working, Slow and Can’t reach mean?

Working means the service answered in under 3 seconds. Slow means it answered, but took 3 seconds or longer. Can’t reach means there was no answer within 10 seconds, or an error came back. Slow or unreachable usually means pages will load or save slowly, or not at all, on this device.

Why is only one service marked Can’t reach?

Each service uses its own address. A school filter, a browser extension or a firewall can block one address and let the others through. Work through the steps above, then give the list of addresses to school IT.

Does this page save anything about me?

No. The checks don’t include your account, your class or anything you’ve entered, and nothing from them is saved. The small bars only cover this visit and clear when you leave the page.

Does this page show past outages?

No. It shows what this device can reach now, plus the last few checks from this visit. Known problems and planned downtime are listed under Notices.
